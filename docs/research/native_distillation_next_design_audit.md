# Conditional next-design audit: blind turns with teacher supervision

**Design review only, 6 November 2026. No new experiment, training extension or protocol change is authorized by this note.** Complete and analyze both fixed distillation endpoints and their already specified geometry screen first. This note considers what a different, separately specified study could address if that pilot improves imitation without developing a useful attracting angular memory.

**The useful change would be supervision, not a new turning task.** Brief blind reorientation, labelled native turn actions, an identical final action suffix, a real forward probe and cue withdrawal are already specified in the [blind-reorientation proposal](rl_blind_reorientation_proposal.md). The [implemented local-return learner](../../scripts/local_goal_return_training.py) uses this family of preparations. Its [learning review](local_return_learning_review.md) identifies sparse reward discovery, credit assignment and entropy pressure as plausible learning difficulties. The current [native policy distillation](../../scripts/native_policy_distillation.py) supplies dense full-vision teacher action probabilities. Combining that teaching signal with a brief reorientation interval would test whether the same original controller can learn the necessary history-dependent computation more readily. It would be a new continuation with a reused physical task idea; success would describe that continuation.

**A blind turn creates a sharper angular-history demand only if the competing information routes are controlled.** During the current blackout pilot, fresh ordinary position codes remain available. Comparing successive positions can reveal travel direction during translation. Native turn actions also do not instantly remove translational or rotational inertia. A prospective reorientation task would therefore need explicitly declared braking boundaries or audited residual motion, held ordinary position input during the turn, actual executed previous-action labels, and matched final action suffixes. Preserve the actual saved goal code. The [existing paired-history necessity audit](../../results/lstm_blind_turn_necessity/README.md) gives the relevant check: two histories reach the same position and motor state with identical complete current inputs but different body headings, for which appropriate goalward actions differ. Independently verify that the full-vision teacher supplies distinguishable, useful action distributions for those cases before committing to a new training recipe.

**Restore ordinary navigation after measuring the ambiguous choices.** A smaller departure from the original task could end the short imposed turn with a first-choice or short fixed choice window, then restore normal spatial feedback and ordinary goal return. Report that window separately from later episode score. Once fresh current codes resume during translation, a controller can reacquire direction through position changes; subsequent success then mixes retained history with reacquisition. Retained versus erased recurrent state, the visible scheduled-erasure reference, and the identical-input paired histories distinguish these routes. Longer held-code navigation would reproduce the additional blind path-integration burden of the earlier local-return task. The task's forced periods, free choices, teaching losses and complete temporal credit window would need a separate fixed specification.

**Body heading and the predictive cue's direction must remain separate.** The native predictive displacement follows the last actual travel direction; it does not rotate with body heading during a stationary turn. A continuously available PGC pattern can therefore be stale as a heading reference. Pure blind turning chiefly encourages visual/action-based heading integration. It does not by itself give a fresh predictive correction. The [existing cue-information analysis](local_return_cue_information.md) and [physical implementation](rl_blind_reorientation_implementation.md) already provide the appropriate correction opportunity: a short genuine forward translation, a native code computed from its measured travel direction, and a matched cue replacing only the predictive current entries with ordinary values at the same endpoint. Withdraw that cue again and measure its later influence. Both populations retain their native goal message. Direction from comparing ordinary endpoint positions remains a valid competing computation and belongs in the control, rather than being removed from the comparison after seeing results.

**The mechanistic sequence stays simple:** establish a useful dependence on history in matched-input navigation; identify what the recurrent state retains; measure whether disturbances away from the angular family recover while angular differences persist; then test whether the brief predictive cue repairs the state and behavior after it is withdrawn. A learned counter, Cartesian estimate or short sequence trace could solve the same behavior. The already specified full-state and fixed-output geometry tests decide which computation appears. No ring-shaped representation, angular training target, favourable checkpoint selection or altered success criterion is proposed here. The present [fixed endpoint geometry protocol](native_policy_distillation_memory_commands.md) remains unchanged.
