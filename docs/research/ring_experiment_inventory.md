## Statistical and numerical checks

Training seeds, not time points or simulated episodes, are the independent units in cohort tests. Planned seed sets and incomplete runs are checked before final aggregation. Each family states its own correction scope; no claim is made that these separate families constitute one global test across the whole research program.

The focused verification suite covers derivatives, known-ring and collapsing-state controls, timing, information isolation, restoration identities, matched perturbation geometry, source hashes, checkpoint identity, budget guards, and statistical aggregation. The final combined focused suite passed **85 tests**. Per-model geometry evaluation additionally verifies exact derivative propagation against independent automatic differentiation.

Large checkpoints and state arrays are excluded from ordinary Git history; compact source data, protocols, numerical summaries and figures are included. The cloud ledger records the source snapshots and locations of trained artifacts. External published checkpoints have a separately verified source manifest.
