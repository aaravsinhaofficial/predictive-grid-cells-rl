# Prospective review: learn a useful directional memory first

**Recommendation: one bounded local goal-return study, with a behavioral gate before expensive dynamics.** This is a design review, not a launch authorization or a claim that a ring will emerge. The completed original-agent, 40M and 90M results remain intact.

The accepted trial accounting, baseline gate and final behavioral gate are sealed with the explicit evaluation plans/tapes in the [prospective bank protocol](../../results/local_goal_return_design/behavior_protocol.json). No policy or environment was executed when those banks were generated.

The earlier task paid mostly for visible navigation: 98.64% of the final 40M PGC agent's goals occurred outside the intended blind challenge. The [local preflight](../../results/rl_blind_reorientation_review/local_goal_preflight_v2/README.md) removes that alternative and verifies physical opportunity. Its heading-aware planner reaches 128/128 targets, while the certified common-information ceiling is 50%. Uniform per-update actions reach 0/128, however. Physical solvability therefore needs a curriculum that exposes the learner to actual early rewards.

## The smallest defensible training change

Use one complete local-return trial as the episode. Preserve the square arena, rendered room, analytic maps, six native actions, 256-unit LSTM, goal radius and +10 reward for the first actual goal hit. The setup pickup supplies the genuine saved goal code but contributes no scored reward, policy observation or gradient. Start the trial with zero h/c, previous action −1 and previous reward zero. End at the first free-phase hit or after 30 free actions; both are true task terminals with zero value bootstrap. There is no later visible phase to pay for waiting.

Use the already checked sequence: 15 visible forced turns, two action-labelled blind turn blocks, four actual forward-probe actions, one endpoint cue as the first delay input, a common forced LEFT delay, then 30 actor-controlled blind actions. Hold the ordinary endpoint position snapshot throughout the delay and free interval, and keep the saved goal snapshot unchanged. The native cue encodes actual probe travel direction. All other cue-branch inputs must match exactly. Preparation follows the explicitly controlled noiseless physics used by the certificate; free motion retains native motor noise. Physical initialization, brakes and endpoint rounding are recorded.

Reset **only the final action and value readouts**, using the repository's native initialization: orthogonal action weights with gain 0.01, value gain 1, and zero biases. Use the same dedicated head-initialization RNG within each matched Grid/PGC pair, preserve every original 1B CNN/LSTM tensor exactly, and then train all weights. The small action weights are nonzero, so actor gradients can reach recurrent features immediately. Their measured initial probabilities should be close to uniform; report the actual entropy and action distribution. Initialize a fresh optimizer and document any zero-learning-rate accumulator warmup separately.

This directly removes the measured inherited turning bias and the old long-episode value estimate. It is **transfer of trained spatial/recurrent features with new action/value readouts**, not continuation of the unchanged original policy. Keep ordinary 15 Hz action decisions and entropy coefficient 0.01. Extra action holds or temperature schedules would add control/exploration changes and are unnecessary for this first design. Retain the previously specified 0.02 blind h/c training noise with exact replay; geometry and primary clean evaluation use noise off, with a separately reported matched-noise sensitivity.

The fixed budget is **1,024 batches of 32 complete trials per stage**, four stages, or **131,072 trials per model**. Count active physical updates × 4 as frames; padding is not experience. Report free decisions, complete trials, warmup exposure and padding separately. Pre-draw each trial's parameters and tapes independently of previous early hits. Keep the complete trial inside a differentiable unroll of at most 160 updates; mask policy/entropy losses on forced actions and all losses on padding, while retaining recurrent credit through real forced updates. Stages are fixed rather than chosen from favorable learning curves. The final endpoint is primary, and every stage is retained.

Sample continuous endpoint positions in the central ±0.30 m square, independent goal bearings and body-heading offsets, randomized room appearance, and independent pickup directions. One genuine saved goal snapshot must be shared across heading variants in a physical context. Near starts need their own preflight: the backward extent of the four-action probe can put an earlier pose inside the goal even when its endpoint is outside. Admit trials only by declared physical wall/goal clearance; report every rejected setup and the resulting angle coverage. No neural result may determine admission.

**Prelaunch learning-opportunity check:** use a fixed small bank at stages 1 and 4, the actual reset-head networks, uniform actions and the analysis-only physical planner. Record every trial, baseline entropy, six action counts, free path, genuine free rewards and forced-phase hits. Require nonzero, broadly distributed stage-1 rewards rather than an isolated lucky example before spending training compute. A practical proposed threshold is at least 5% stage-1 success for each reset-head population over 256 trials, with zero forced rewards; freeze the bank and threshold before running it. If this fails, revise the start distribution before training and preserve the failed preflight. The final hard bank retains the independent path/information certificate; the 50% ceiling is not claimed for easy stages.

## Behavioral gate and stopping rule

Before training outcomes, seal a final hard-task bank of **16 physical contexts × 16 headings = 256 trials**, including antipodal pairs with shared body-frame noise/action tapes. Verify the v2 common-input, motor-state, wall-clearance and path-length certificate on every saved tape. A certificate failure remains reported; do not apply the 50% theorem to an uncertified trial or replace trials after seeing policy outcomes. The conservative aggregate bound is 0.5 times the certified-pair fraction plus 1 times the uncertified fraction. The special 50% criterion requires every pair to be certified.

At every fixed endpoint, evaluate intact state, post-cue h/c erasure, ordinary cue, and ±60° wrong cues on the same bank. Retain failures and use first-hit success as the primary behavioral measure. State erasure occurs immediately after the cue and leaves all physical/input variables intact. Also retain continuous progress, action probabilities and path length.

**Proposed pilot gate:** final hard-task intact success at least **65%**, and post-cue erasure cost at least **15 percentage points**, in at least one population. These are descriptive engineering thresholds for deciding whether there is a useful memory to investigate, not a significance test or a fitted optimum. Report both populations regardless of which qualifies. If neither qualifies, finish the fixed pilot and examine reward discovery, free-action entropy and gradient/return accounting; do not automatically launch hundreds of geometry assays or extend training. If only Grid qualifies, that is an informative outcome for the predictive-cue hypothesis.

Erasure changes the entire recurrent state and can change strategy or general readiness. The next, narrower causal test therefore replaces the state with the state from a matched **±30° physical-history trial**, immediately **before** the endpoint cue. Regenerate those actual histories or use a separately frozen dense family: the 16-angle behavioral bank has 22.5° spacing and cannot supply a 30° shift by relabeling a neighbor. Preserve the recipient's actual physical state, inputs and correct cue. Compare correct, ordinary and ±60° wrong pulses; every other input and the subsequent physical rollout are identical. Track actual travel direction separately from body heading as the latter rotates during the LEFT delay. Both signs and every context are retained. Measure the direction of resulting action/movement changes, paired goal progress/success and phase repair after the cue has disappeared. A rescue claim requires a persistent, correctly directed effect beyond the unperturbed cue contrast, with radial and matched random state edits as controls.

Only after the behavioral gate, apply the existing full-state and prespecified future-hidden/actor-score geometry tests to fixed task-native histories. Preserve the distinction between a recurrent ring under common ordinary input, transverse recovery around its moving trajectory, and phase correction caused by a brief predictive pulse. A circle-shaped decoding plot is not the decision criterion. The task can also be solved by a changing continuous trace or a more categorical action memory; those are legitimate measured alternatives.

## Replication and scope

Use source seed 1 as the bounded implementation/feasibility pilot. Seed 2 is a separately identified development replication if changes are needed. Freeze the final task, behavioral gates, cue timing, geometry maps and endpoints before **source seeds 3–8** are trained as six matched confirmation pairs. Include every confirmation seed and failed learner; average trials within model and compare effects across paired models. Contexts, headings and cue variants are repeated measurements, not independent trained networks. A pilot that learns memory alone is not a population claim about PGCs.

This study asks whether a memory-relevant task can train a useful directional mechanism from the repository's original features, and whether predictive inputs repair or improve that mechanism. It does not retroactively attribute the original 1B navigation advantage to the newly learned mechanism.

## Primary literature used

- **Florensa et al. (CoRL 2017):** reverse curricula obtain sparse goal rewards from feasible starts near a goal and expand outward. Their algorithm generates nearby states with actions and retains intermediate-success starts (10–90%); this proposal borrows the start-distribution principle and uses a simpler fixed schedule. It preserves the actual goal reward. [Paper](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v78/florensa17a.html).
- **Heess et al. (2015):** recurrent policy gradients with LSTMs solved targets visible only initially, return-to-start control and a water maze requiring return to a previously found platform. Feedforward controls failed the memory component. This supports an actual return-navigation task and behavior-based memory controls; it supplies no ring guarantee. [Primary paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/1512.04455).
- **Song, Yang and Wang (eLife 2017):** actor–critic reward training learned parametric working memory with a variable 2.7–3.3 s delay and other cognitive tasks. This supports learning memory from action rewards without supervised neural or angle trajectories. Its networks and tasks differ from this navigation LSTM. [Primary paper](https://elifesciences.org/articles/21492).
- **Ni, Eysenbach and Salakhutdinov (ICML 2022):** recurrent model-free RL can perform well on diverse partially observed tasks, with implementation and context length affecting outcomes. Their main methods use different off-policy actor–critic designs; we borrow the requirement to audit history, masking and credit, rather than silently replacing the repository's learner. [Paper](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v162/ni22a.html).

No new training, cloud instance or source change was performed for this review.
