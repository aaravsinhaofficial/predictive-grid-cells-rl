# Direction-drive intervention confirmation

Frozen before inspecting any subspace intervention outcome from confirmation seeds 2001–2016. The pilot checkpoint is predictive_s1006 at 6,000 training updates. All 16 confirmation predictive checkpoints enter, irrespective of training quality, ring geometry, or intervention success. This is a prespecified follow-up within the ongoing investigation, not a preregistered independent study.

For each frozen model separately, fit the learned encoder difference between observed predictive and same-position ordinary grid inputs to an intercept plus cos(direction) and sin(direction). Use 1,024 independent positions in [−1.25, 1.25]² and 32 uniformly spaced directions with independent position-wise phase offsets, random seed 517913. Set measured speed/angular velocity to zero, spatial availability to one, and initial heading cue to zero. Exclude the intercept and take the two direction coefficient vectors' span as the primary rank-2 subspace. The projector is fixed thereafter. No trajectory or intervention outcome selects the subspace.

The same RNG then generates two test sets of 256 new positions × 32 directions: one in the same region and one shifted 10 m along x. Report harmonic regression variance explained, within-position directional variance explained, projected drive energy, and circular readout error. Harmonics 1–3 form a separately labeled fit-only secondary diagnostic; they cannot replace the primary subspace after seeing outcomes.

Interventions use only the pair of encoder drives, with no true heading, position, or desired answer passed to the intervention: native; ordinary; native minus the projected difference; ordinary plus the projected difference; and ordinary plus the residual difference. The last and the deletion intervention are algebraically equivalent and are checked numerically. Three fixed rank-2 random subspaces (seed 517914), orthogonal to the fitted subspace, supply deletion and restoration controls matched to each input's projected-drive norm. All original weights stay frozen.

Static anchoring uses six fixed positions, 72 evenly spaced headings, common four-step native cue initialization, 40 blank updates, and ±10° actual neighboring ring states. Follow all branches for 200 updates (10 s). At 5 s, report correct-heading error, circle coverage, state radius, and both full-state and decoded phase perturbation retention. Contraction alone is insufficient: contraction into a single wrong state counts as collapse, not useful anchoring. The first five positions form the primary static summary; the sixth, [10, 10] m, is a transfer check.

Diagnostic amendment before any confirmation checkpoint analysis: also report median, 10th percentile, and 90th percentile of the per-state absolute phase-error gain. Exclude initial phase separations at or below 10⁻⁵ radians and report the valid fraction. This addresses the difference between a pooled ratio and the distribution of local effects. It does not change the fitted plane, interventions, random controls, or primary functional outcomes. Figures pair correction with correct-heading accuracy and circle coverage. The native/ordinary input pair is supplied by the experiment; it is not assumed to be two separate sensory channels naturally available to the unmodified model.

Forced trajectories use seed 931731, 256 episodes of 256 steps, common native four-step initialization, and no added recurrent noise. Test both the native region and a 10 m x displacement with the same sensor realization. The primary functional outcome is mean absolute heading error from step 8 onward at new places. Report same-region results, visible/blank epochs, and final error as secondary measurements.

The training seed is the inferential unit. Primary paired contrasts at new places are native vs ordinary, removal vs native, ordinary vs restoration, and restoration vs the mean of the three random restorations. Report all 16 paired values, mean differences with 95% t intervals, exact paired sign-flip p values and Holm correction within these four contrasts. Do not omit unsuccessful models. Static anchoring and random deletion controls support interpretation and remain separately labeled secondary analyses.

Validation includes native/ordinary full-state equality against the untouched model, deletion/residual equivalence, projector idempotence, equal sham dose, zero intervention during blank observations, and unchanged checkpoint hashes. The exact executed source and fitting projectors are archived per model.

Validation-only amendment before confirmation analysis: embed the originating checkpoint SHA and model seed in the fitted projector archive, and require exact agreement before navigation; assert independent navigation equality for success and arrival (tolerance 10⁻⁶ s) and final/self-position distances (tolerance 10⁻⁴ m). The pilot values were exactly equal before adding these assertions. Sham energy is matched in the encoder preactivation drive, not necessarily in post-tanh state-update size or readout sensitivity.

Navigation scope was fixed after the pilot completed in 20.9 seconds, before any confirmation navigation outcomes: all 16 models, 128 matched episodes per model, seed 836219, 40 seconds, in both the native region and a 10 m x displacement. Twenty-second outcomes are nested secondary measurements. Use the existing estimator/controller/physical simulator, common native four-step initialization, the same per-step observation and motor noise, and the same ten unique interventions (native, ordinary, direction deletion/restoration, and three random deletions/restorations). The controller receives only decoded heading, estimated position, and the known goal. All branches have their own physical trajectories. Do not freeze the physical path across navigation interventions. The primary navigation outcome is first arrival within 0.15 m of the goal by 40 s at new places, with four paired contrasts analogous to the forced-heading family and their own Holm correction. Native-region outcomes, final distance, position-estimate error, sustained holding and 20 s results are secondary. Independently rerun the untouched navigation evaluator for native/ordinary identity in every model. Archive per-episode outcomes, exact sources, and source/checkpoint hashes.
