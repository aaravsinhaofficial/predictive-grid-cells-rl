# Fixed mean versus varying direction-plane drive

This is an additional mechanistic diagnostic requested after the rank-2 pilot and after the first six confirmation models had their frozen rank-2 analyses computed. It does not alter the original subspace projection, calibration sets, endpoints, or four-plus-four corrected test families. All 16 predictive checkpoints (seeds 2001–2016) enter regardless of outcomes. This is a locally specified follow-up, not an external preregistration.

For each model, use the saved independent calibration array from the frozen rank-2 study: 1,024 positions × 32 directions, seed 517913. Define μ as its mean encoder-drive difference, accumulated in float64; retain the original fixed rank-2 projector P. Do not fit anything to intervention outcomes. Report the fraction `||Pμ||² / mean(||PΔb||²)` of projected-drive energy due to this global mean.

Let `a` be the observed spatial-availability flag. Define the fixed tonic component `m=aPμ` and the varying component `v=PΔb−m`. The added conditions are ordinary drive plus m (mean-only restoration), and native drive minus v (mean-preserving removal). This availability gate makes both interventions exact no-ops during blackouts. With a visible spatial cue, these reduce to `bO+Pμ` and `bN−P(Δb−μ)`. Inputs are observed paired encoder drives, the availability flag, and a fixed calibration mean. No true heading, position, or direction label enters the intervention.

Compare against native, ordinary, and the full-plane restoration `bO+PΔb`. Keep all old calibration parameters, trial seeds, sample sizes, and initialization conventions: 72-phase anchoring at six positions for 200 updates, primary static report at 100 updates across the first five positions; 256 forced trajectories × 256 steps using seed 931731, excluding the first eight steps; and 128 closed-loop navigation episodes using seed 836219, 40 seconds, in both native and 10 m translated places. Baseline and all variants share the native four-step initialization. Each navigation branch controls its own physical path. Added recurrent noise is zero. Score correct-heading error, coverage, full-state retention, and navigation; contraction alone is not evidence of useful stabilization.

All 16 seed-level values and paired mean differences with nominal 95% t intervals will be reported. Three additional exploratory contrasts are fixed for each endpoint at translated places: mean-only benefit over ordinary; full-plane benefit over mean-only; and cost of mean-preserving removal relative to native. Exact paired sign-flip p values receive Holm correction within the three heading contrasts and separately within the three navigation contrasts. These diagnostics do not enter or change the original primary test families.

Validate the drive decomposition, zero effects during blackouts, binding to the originating checkpoint and calibration artifact, untouched source/checkpoint hashes, and independent native/ordinary navigation identities. The imported navigation simulator's condition list is configured only inside the new diagnostic process; no frozen source file or concurrent evaluation is changed. The global calibration mean tests a fixed offset. It does not remove every position-dependent average or match the nonlinear network's response to fluctuations in drive magnitude.
