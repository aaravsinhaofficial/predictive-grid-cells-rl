This release contains the completed fixed policy-distillation pilot and its prospective direction-memory tests in the original navigation architecture.

Only existing LSTM weights changed. In the fixed seed-1 pair, blackout scores rose from 32.5 to 42.5 for Grid and 15.0 to 31.875 for PGC. Exactly matched recorded histories show improved teacher imitation and a useful recurrent-state contribution. All six behavior/replay conditions, 16 held-out behavior episodes per condition and 32 replay episodes per model are retained.

All 24 planned geometry contexts completed. The matched two-second screen nominates 0/36 final tests; final own-policy contexts nominate 0/36. There are no individual nominations among all 720 metric/time/centering rows. Output errors contract more strongly after training, while direction families also shrink and deform. These are one-seed exploratory continuations, not a population confirmation or a demonstration of ring stabilization.
